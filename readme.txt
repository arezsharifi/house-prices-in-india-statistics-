## Overview

Statistical analysis of the factors affecting house prices in India,
based on a dataset of 479 properties and 22 variables.

## Key Findings

- **Living area** is the strongest positive predictor of price.
- **Basement presence** raises mean price by roughly 40%.
- **Grade of the house** correlates nearly linearly with price.
- **Condition = 3** is an interesting outlier (old but valuable houses).
- **Lot area** has negligible effect — a cultural/geographical difference
  from markets like Iran.
- **Latitude/Longitude** show a localized price hotspot around
  lat 52.7–52.9 and long -114.3 to -114.5.
